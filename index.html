Simulator

In development

A PC flight simulator built to teach, not just to fly — tune real aerodynamic variables and watch how the aircraft responds.

Learn

Aerodynamics
You will learn how angle of attack changes lift and drag, and where that curve breaks into a stall
Flight physics
Forces computed on a real Rigidbody, with PID-based control keeping the aircraft stable in flight
Engineering
Experiment with wing design, airfoils and aircraft configuration. Change the shape, feel how the handling changes

Roadmap

  1. 01 Flight physics core — manually-computed aerodynamics on a Rigidbody, PID-based rate control with attitude hold
  2. 02 Flyable aircraft, HUD, and terrain — flight path marker, pitch ladder, landing gear, procedural terrain
  3. 03Guided flight tutorial and refined handling
  4. 04Aircraft-building sandbox — design your own wing and airfoil configuration
  5. 05Multiple planets and atmospheric conditions, plus rocketry

FAQ

What is Aerolab?

A free, educational project to learn aeronautics and science by experimenting with real variables in a flight simulator.

Is it free?

Yes. Aerolab is a non-commercial, educational project.

What platforms will it support?

PC first. Other platforms may follow depending on how the project grows.
