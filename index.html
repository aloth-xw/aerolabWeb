FAQ

What is Aerolab?

A free, educational project to learn aeronautics and science by experimenting with real variables in a flight simulator.

Is it free?

Yes. Aerolab is a non-commercial, educational project.

What platforms will it support?

PC first. Other platforms may follow depending on how the project grows.
